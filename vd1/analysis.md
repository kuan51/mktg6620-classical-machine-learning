### Value scenarios for the same fixed list

Formula: net per 1,000 contacts = 1,000 × (r × s × $66 − $6.20). Break-even s = $6.20 / (r × $66).
All inputs are fictional planning assumptions; these are not measured savings.

| Method | r (list churn rate) | s = 10% | s = 15% | s = 20% | Break-even s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contract rule | 39.9% | −$3,569.40 | −$2,254.09 | −$938.79 | 23.6% |
| Logistic | 69.4% | −$1,619.93 | +$670.11 | +$2,960.14 | 13.5% |
| **Boosted trees (chosen)** | **69.4%** | **−$1,619.93** | **+$670.11** | **+$2,960.14** | **13.5%** |

For the actual 281-call list: total call cost 281 × $6.20 = $1,742.20 for every method. Trees net at s = 10/15/20%:
−$455.20 / +$188.30 / +$831.80. Cost per real leaver reached: trees and logistic $8.93, contract $15.56.

**Independent arithmetic check (trees, s = 15%):**
r = 195 / 281 = 0.693950. 0.693950 × 0.15 × 66 = 6.8701. 6.8701 − 6.20 = 0.6701. × 1,000 = **+$670.11**, matching `outputs/scenarios.csv`.
Break-even: 6.20 / (0.693950 × 66) = 6.20 / 45.80 = **13.5%**.
